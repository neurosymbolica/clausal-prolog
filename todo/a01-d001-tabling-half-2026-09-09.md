# A01-D001, tabling half: answer dedup still collapses Decimal(1) with 1

Filed 2026-09-09 while specifying standard order of terms. The operator ruled the
comparison site in scope and this half out, to keep a C rebuild out of a branch about
term ordering.

## The residual has two sites

1. `clausal/logic/builtins/iso_compare.py::_numeric_tag` — the COMPARISON site. Fixed by
   `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-09-standard-order-of-terms-design.md` §4a: `Decimal`
   and `Fraction` become their own kinds, so `'=='` is transitive and `compare/3` can hold
   the ISO identity `compare(=, X, Y) <=> X == Y`.
2. `clausal/logic/tabling.py::_normalize_for_key_py` and its C twin
   `_tabling_core.c::do_normalize` — the TABLING site. NOT fixed. Both leave
   `Decimal`/`Fraction` raw, so they still conflate with `int` in variant keys and answer
   dedup.

## The inconsistency this leaves

After the comparison fix, `'=='` says `Decimal(1)` and `1` are two distinct terms, while a
tabled predicate yielding both keeps only one answer. Two mechanisms disagree about how
many terms exist.

Exotic in practice — it needs a tabled predicate producing a Decimal and an int of equal
value as separate answers — which is why it was ruled out of scope rather than fixed.

## What fixing it involves

Tag both numeric types in `_normalize_for_key_py`, mirror the change in the C twin
`do_normalize`, keep P52 lock-step (the two MUST produce identical keys), rebuild the
extension, and run the tabling parity corpus. Note `_normalize_for_key_py` builds
`(type, value)` pairs for tagged numbers — these are HASH KEYS only, never passed to
arithmetic.

asked about `unify` rather than about keys.
